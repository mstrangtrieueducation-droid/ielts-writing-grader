four-paragraph revision you produce. Do not claim the student has two Detail
paragraphs just because the revised essay should have two. An unannotated

the role is uncertain, use the actual paragraph number. Never put Unit, unit_id,
internal checklist IDs, or a sentence-audit segment number in Correction.section.

quotes must locate every correction; the UI independently derives the displayed
paragraph/sentence position from those quotes in the unchanged original.
